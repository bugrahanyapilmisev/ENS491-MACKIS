   following criteria:   - Obtain an "Acceptance Letter" from the host
   organization for a minimum internship period of 2 months. - Have a GPA of
   at least 2.20 for undergraduates or 2.5 for graduate students. - Complete

   procedures defined in the Erasmus+ Outgoing Student Procedure
   (PIPAR-C710-0201) and submit their documents to the Internship Office. -
   For undergraduate students, ENG 101-102 course grades or SPS and AL course
   grades are calculated, and language scores are determined based on the
   equivalence table of Foreign Language Exams recommended by the Council of
   Higher Education. - Graduate/Doctoral students' TOEFL/YDS/ELAE results are
   converted according to the same table. - Each student's Erasmus+ Score is
   calculated by taking 50% of the calculated language score and 50% of the
   GPA. - The Internship Office lists students in order of highest Erasmus+
   Score. - The application requirements and necessary documents are
   published on the internal web and internship website starting at the end
   of October. - Students must submit their application documents (CV,
